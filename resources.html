Phone-Free Schools

Phone-free, bell-to-bell schools give kids a real break from their devices during the school day. The Phone Free Schools Movement is a great place to start, and PA Unplugged's one-pagers cover what these policies are, why they work, and how to implement them well.

The Research

Key study 🧠

Brain Drain: The Mere Presence of One's Own Smartphone

Landmark experiment showing that simply having a smartphone nearby, even silenced and face-down, measurably reduces working memory and focus. (Ward, Duke, Gneezy & Bos, Journal of the Association for Consumer Research, 2017)

Read the study → 📲

Smartphone Use During School Hours (ABCD Study)

National data on how much U.S. adolescents use their phones during the school day, from the Adolescent Brain Cognitive Development study.

Read the study → 🩺

CHOP Smartphone Study (Pediatrics, 2025)

Children's Hospital of Philadelphia research, published in Pediatrics, on adolescent smartphone use and wellbeing.
