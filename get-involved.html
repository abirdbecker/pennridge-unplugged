Get Involved

Pennridge Unplugged is a community effort, and there are lots of ways to be part of it. Stay informed, connect with other families, share your perspective, or help create change in our community.

Our advocacy and relationship-building are focused on Pennridge schools, and we welcome anyone who lives within the district to join our meetings, mailing list, and events. We hope this can be a space to connect, learn, share ideas, and contribute to a more intentional technology culture in our community. There's a place for everyone in this conversation.

Be Informed

Stay connected to what's happening in Pennridge and beyond.

  • Join our mailing list for news, resources, upcoming events, and ways to participate.
  • Attend an upcoming community event or conversation.
  • Come to one of our monthly meetings, held on the third Thursday of the month from 6:30 to 8 PM, to learn more about what we're working on.
  • Follow along as we share ideas and opportunities for more intentional technology use at home, at school, and in our community.

Be Involved

Connect with other families and add your voice to the conversation.

  • Take the PA Unplugged parent survey and contribute to statewide data about families' experiences with technology in schools.
  • Connect with other families at your elementary school, commit to delaying smartphones and social media until after 8th grade with the Wait Until 8th pledge, and find local support along the way.
  • Talk with us about what you're seeing in your family, school, or community, and what you'd like to see change.

Take Action

Help create a community where kids have more opportunities for connection, independence, play, and life beyond screens.

There are many ways to take action, and we're always looking for people with ideas, connections, and energy to contribute. You might:

  • Build community: Help organize an event, conversation, or family activity; bring Pennridge Unplugged to your school, neighborhood, or community group; or help connect families with phone-free and low-tech opportunities.
  • Partner locally: Work with schools, organizations, and businesses to create spaces and practices that support intentional technology use.
  • Speak up: Attend school board meetings and share your perspective through public comment, connect with school and district leaders, or advocate at the county and state levels for policies that support healthy technology use and children's well-being.
  • Contribute your skills: Share information and resources, make connections, or bring your ideas and experience to a project that interests you.

Want to find your place?

Reach out to us. Tell us what you're interested in, what questions you have, or what you'd like to help with. We'd love to talk about ways to plug you into what's already happening, or work together to create something new.
